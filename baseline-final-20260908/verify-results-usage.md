Output is JSON, schema version 1:

- `evidence_status`: `PASS` for complete internally consistent evidence; `FAIL` for missing/corrupt/inconsistent evidence. Integrity failures exit **1**, set `scientific_status=NOT_EVALUATED`, and prohibit eligibility.
- `scientific_status` and `equal_output_speedup_eligible`: authoritative full-PSD, strain, geometry, trigger and within-arm timing-output verdict. Legitimate scientific `FAIL` exits **0** with valid evidence.
- `comparisons`: five qualification comparisons, preserved/recomputed raw and normalized results, conditioning metrics, individual `eligible` flags and descriptive median wall ratios. Only independently validated `source_snapshot` and `consumed_input_sha256` are normalized. Only the two display paths relocate when validating archived comparator output.
- Each `conditioning` reports `full_psd_pass`, `in_band_psd_budget_pass`, `in_band_psd_exact`, and five segment records containing full-array masks, dtype/shape, errors and finite-violation bounds. PSD tolerance stays **rtol 1e-4, atol 0**. Exact used bins cannot override full-array failure.
- `arms`, `timing_comparisons`, `schedule`: receipt-derived samples, medians, 384×1904/median rates, and all 16 comparisons against each arm's qualification.
- A completed timing case must compare **PASS** against its own qualification. A failing/review verdict is an evidence error because the frozen controller could not mark that case completed. Permitted cross-arm scientific failures remain scientific failures.
- `timing_consistency`: independent GNU elapsed, enclosing receipt and runtime spans for all 20 processes, their differences and explicit allowances. These check consistency; they do not adjust measured durations/rates or estimate timing uncertainty.
- `dependency_reconciliation`: the preserved manifest version, observed worker version, frozen post-run diagnostic/script hashes, acquisition binding and retrospective limitations described below.
- `continuation`, `acquired_summary`, `input_evidence_sha256`: preserved failure/policy, acquired-summary hash and independently identified eligibility differences, plus hashes of inspected evidence.

Source/input bytes are checked when transferred. Otherwise provenance rests on the recorded pins and imported-module receipts; remote reconstruction is not claimed. Optional `committed-source-verification.json` is checked against its frozen review hash and commit/count fields, but that receipt contains no per-file manifest hash and does not replace independent archive verification. Conditioned strain equality uses saved digests/metadata because acquisition did not save strain samples.

GNU elapsed parsing accepts `m:ss` and `h:mm:ss`, with fractional seconds where present. One full displayed unit covers rounding/truncation (0.01 s for hundredths; 1 s for integer seconds). Fixed conservative consistency bounds are 1 s for launch/receipt-save/wait scheduling, 5 s for interpreter startup/final receipt/shutdown outside the runtime timestamps, and 60 s for enclosing monitoring overhead: two GPU queries each bounded by 15 s, a 20 s monitor join, and 10 s for other sampling/I/O. Timestamp containment permits 0.05 s for precision/small wall-clock adjustments. These are explicit review bounds derived from producer boundaries, not fitted performance thresholds or guaranteed overhead maxima; violations require investigation. Runtime spans must be nested inside receipt spans, and perf/GNU durations must agree with both.
